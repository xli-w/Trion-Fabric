# Stage 12 — Delivery, Benefits and Continuous Improvement

## Objective

Connect the diagnostic and roadmap to actual implementation and measured outcomes.

The platform should help Trion follow an improvement from recommendation to realised result.

## Delivery model

Implement a coherent relationship:

Opportunity → Initiative → Milestones → Actions → Measurement → Outcome → Lessons Learned

Do not turn the platform into a generic enterprise project-management system.

## Initiative workspace

For each initiative, support:

- Objective.
- Related opportunity.
- Related finding.
- Current problem.
- Scope.
- Owner.
- Status.
- Timeline.
- Milestones.
- Actions.
- Dependencies.
- Prerequisites.
- Risks.
- Estimated cost.
- Expected benefits.
- Actual benefits.
- Review status.
- Internal notes.
- Approved client summary.

## Benefits model

Support:

- Benefit type.
- Measure.
- Baseline.
- Target.
- Expected value.
- Actual value.
- Unit.
- Measurement method.
- Measurement owner.
- Measurement date.
- Confidence.
- Status.
- Notes.

Keep expected and actual benefits separate.

Support benefit categories such as:

- Productivity.
- Cost.
- Quality.
- Delivery.
- Capacity.
- Visibility.
- Risk.
- Customer service.
- Other.

Do not mark a benefit as realised merely because an initiative is complete.

## Dependencies and sequencing

Make dependencies visible between:

- Opportunities.
- Initiatives.
- Milestones.
- Actions.

Support foundational improvements, such as:

- Process standardisation.
- Master-data improvement.
- KPI definition.
- Data ownership.
- ERP optimisation.
- Removal of duplicate sources.
- Integration preparation.

Do not automatically rank foundational work as low priority.

## Lessons learned

After an initiative or engagement, capture:

- What worked.
- What did not work.
- What changed.
- What benefit was realised.
- What should be repeated.
- What should be avoided.
- Reusable internal knowledge.
- Follow-up recommendations.

Ensure client-specific information is not automatically added to reusable knowledge without review.

## Acceptance criteria

- Opportunities can become delivery initiatives.
- Delivery progress is visible.
- Dependencies and milestones work.
- Expected and actual benefits are separate.
- Benefits require measurement information.
- Lessons learned can be captured and reviewed.
- The workflow remains connected to the original evidence and rationale.
