# Fabric Product Principles

Fabric should continue to be shaped by the following principles:

1. Evidence before assumption.
2. Structured information before documents.
3. One source of truth for transformation knowledge.
4. Human review before approved conclusions.
5. Practical manufacturing usability over decorative dashboards.
6. Internal working data, draft client-facing material, approved client-facing outputs, and archived records remain distinct.
7. Build explicit seams for AI, persistence, permissions, and reporting rather than hard-coding them into pages.
8. One active client engagement is the centre of normal work; preserve its
   context while moving between fieldwork, analysis, planning, and outputs.
9. Prefer contextual workbenches over a top-level destination for every
   underlying entity.
10. Preserve analytical depth and connected records while removing
    enterprise-style workflow ceremony.
11. Surface a grounded current understanding and the next useful action rather
    than activity counts or decorative dashboards.
12. Keep AI and methodology contextual assistants, not primary product
    destinations.
13. Build for the Trion team that exists: lightweight collaboration,
    permissions, and controlled sharing rather than CRM, portfolio, resource,
    or enterprise project-management behaviour.
